White Water Rafting

White water rafting is an exciting outdoor activity that involves navigating through rough waters in an inflatable raft. It is a thrilling adventure that requires teamwork, skill, and courage.

History

White water rafting has a rich history that dates back to the early 20th century. It began as a recreational activity on rivers and has grown into a popular adventure sport enjoyed by people around the world.
